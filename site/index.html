Questions drivers ask

What is Track Evolution?

Track Evolution is a logbook for HPDE and track-day drivers. It keeps every track, event, session and lap time, charts your best laps at each track over time, reads lap times and telemetry from your own video and data files, and tracks the cars you drive. It runs in any browser at trackevolution.app and as an app for iPhone, iPad, Apple silicon Macs and Android.

Is Track Evolution free?

Yes. The logbook is free with no limit and no expiry: tracks, events, sessions, lap times, progress charts, telemetry import, share links and leaderboards. Track Evolution Pro costs $1.99 a month or $19.99 a year and adds GPS lap recording, the full telemetry analysis, wear tracking, the setup notebook, lap video clips, coach sharing and the AI assistant connection.

Can I time my laps with just a phone?

Yes, with Pro. The iPhone and Android apps time laps from the phone's GPS: start recording, stow the phone, and pick the start/finish line on the track map afterwards. With the phone mounted in view you also get live lap times and a delta. How lap recording works.

Which data loggers and cameras can it import?

Corvette Performance Data Recorder (PDR) video, Cadillac V-Series Blackwing PDR video, GoPro footage with GPS (Hero 5 and newer), AiM SmartyCam video, Racelogic VBO files, including VBOX, RaceChrono, TrackAddict and Harry's LapTimer exports, and Porsche Track Precision VBO and CSV exports. Import is free on the web and in both phone apps. See every supported source.

How do I get lap times out of a Corvette PDR video?

Drop the PDR's MP4 file into an event. Lap times, the racing line, top speed, max RPM and max lateral G are read from the telemetry inside the video. A recording with no lap beacons is timed once you pick the start/finish line on its map. Corvette PDR lap times, step by step.

Is my video uploaded when I import it?

No. Video and data files are read on your own device, in the browser or the phone app, and never uploaded. The one exception is your choice: with Pro you can save a short video clip of a lap.

Does it work offline at the track?

Yes. Your whole logbook works offline on the web, iPhone and Android, and what you add without signal syncs when you are back online.

Can ChatGPT or Claude analyze my lap data?

Yes, with Pro. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to your logbook and ask where the time went against your best lap, what changed since last season or when the brake pads are due. The assistant reads what the app shows and cannot change anything. The web app also has an AI Coach, which debriefs a session or explains the gap between two laps on your own Anthropic or OpenAI account. How to connect an assistant.

What is an HPDE?

HPDE stands for High Performance Driver Education: a track day where you drive your own car on a circuit in run groups sorted by experience, usually with an instructor when you are new. It is not racing: there are no results and no wheel-to-wheel driving. Your first track day covers what you need and where to find an event.

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Nothing you logged is lost. Your events, sessions and lap times stay, you can keep adding more for free, and the Pro features come back if you resubscribe.
