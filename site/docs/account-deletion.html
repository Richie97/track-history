Delete your account

You can have your Track Evolution account and everything in it deleted at any time. Track Evolution (trackevolution.app) is operated by Speedshift LLC; deletion requests are handled by email.

The steps

  1. Download a copy of your logbook if you want one, from Settings → Your data on the web. Deletion can't be undone.
  2. Cancel a Track Evolution Pro subscription bought in the iPhone or Android app, in the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
  3. Email eric@speedshift.io from the address you sign in with, asking for your account to be deleted.

Request deletion

Email eric@speedshift.io asking for your account to be deleted. Send it from the email address you sign in with (your Google or Apple account email), so we can verify the request is yours. If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, say so, and we will match your relay address.

Email a deletion request

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Track Evolution Pro subscription bought in the iPhone or Android app. That subscription is held by the store that sold it, and keeps renewing and charging you until you cancel it there.
  • App Store: on your iPhone or iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Google Play: the Play Store app → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro runs to the end of the term you've paid for. The store handles refunds under its own policy; we can't issue them. If you bought the app when it was a paid download, there's no subscription to cancel.

A subscription bought on the web is the exception. Deleting your account cancels it immediately and deletes your customer record at Stripe, our payment processor, so nothing renews afterwards. You can also cancel it yourself first, from Settings → Subscription → Manage billing.

What gets deleted

Everything. Your account and all its data are permanently deleted:

  • Sign-in details.
  • Tracks, events, sessions, lap times, notes and goals.
  • Imported telemetry results (racing lines and per-lap channel data).
  • Any best-lap videos you saved, removed from storage within a day.
  • Your garage: vehicles, parts, wear measurements and setup sheets.
  • Any share page you created, which stops working immediately.
  • The subscription record we hold (the store's or Stripe's identifier and whether it was active).

Nothing about your account is kept afterwards.

If you connected YouTube to upload best-lap videos, deleting your account also withdraws that permission at Google, within a day, and deletes our record of your uploads. It does not delete videos already on YouTube; Track Evolution never deletes anything there. Delete those yourself in YouTube Studio, before or after.

Deletion can't be undone, so download a copy first. Settings → Your data on the web gives you your laps and events as spreadsheets and the whole logbook as one file (see Your data). Requests are normally handled within 30 days.

You may also want to

  • Revoke Track Evolution's access to your Google account from your Google account settings.
  • Remove Track Evolution's YouTube upload permission on Google's permissions page, if you connected YouTube. Deleting your account also does this, within a day.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple with Track Evolution from account.apple.com under Sign in with Apple.
  • Uninstall the mobile app. Anything cached on your device (offline data, an unsaved lap recording) lives only there and is removed with the app.

To delete only some of your data, no email is needed. Delete individual tracks, events, sessions, laps, vehicles and parts in the app, and turn a share page off in Settings. See the privacy policy for what's stored.
