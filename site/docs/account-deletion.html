Delete your account

You can have your Track Evolution account and everything in it deleted at any time. Track Evolution (trackevolution.app) is operated by Speedshift LLC; deletion requests are handled by email.

Request deletion

Send an email to eric@speedshift.io asking for your account to be deleted. Send it from the email address you sign in with (your Google or Apple account email) — that's how we verify the request is really yours. If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, mention that in the message so we can match your relay address.

Email a deletion request

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Track Evolution Pro subscription. The subscription lives with the store that sold it, not with us, so it keeps renewing and charging you until you cancel it there.
  • App Store: on your iPhone or iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Google Play: the Play Store app → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro runs to the end of the term you've already paid for. Refunds are handled by the store under its own policy — we can't issue them. If you bought the app back when it was a paid download, there's no subscription to cancel.

What gets deleted

Everything. Your account and all data associated with it are permanently deleted: sign-in details, tracks, events, sessions, lap times, notes, goals, imported telemetry results (racing lines and per-lap channel data), your garage — vehicles, parts, wear measurements and setup sheets — any share page you created, which stops working immediately, and the subscription record we hold (the store's transaction identifier and whether it was active). There is no partial retention; nothing about your account is kept afterwards.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone, so copy out anything you want to keep first. Requests are handled promptly — normally within 30 days.

You may also want to

  • Revoke Track Evolution's access to your Google account from your Google account settings.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple with Track Evolution from account.apple.com under Sign in with Apple.
  • Uninstall the mobile app. Anything cached on your device (offline data, an unsaved lap recording) lives only there and is removed with the app.

If you only want to delete some of your data, you don't need to email anyone — individual tracks, events, sessions, laps, vehicles and parts can all be deleted in the app, and a share page can be turned off in Settings. See the privacy policy for the full picture of what's stored.
