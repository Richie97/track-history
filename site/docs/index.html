Getting started

Track Evolution lives at trackevolution.app. Sign in with your Google or Apple account and you get your own logbook — this page walks through logging your first event, importing telemetry, and sharing your progress.

Sign in

Open trackevolution.app and sign in with Google or Apple. There's no separate account to create — a fresh, empty logbook is waiting on first sign-in, and everything you record is private to your account. Both buttons reach the same logbook: accounts are matched by email, so if you sign in with Google one day and Apple the next (using the same address), it's one account.

Free and Pro

The logbook is free, with no limit on how much you log: tracks, events, sessions, lap times, best laps, progress charts, consistency, per-session stats, your cars, prep checklists, share pages and leaderboards — all of it, offline included, on all three apps.

Track Evolution Pro — $1.99/month or $19.99/year — adds the analysis: recording laps from the phone's GPS, telemetry import, per-lap channel graphs with sector splits and lap-vs-lap deltas, consumable wear tracking in the garage, the setup notebook, and year in review. It applies to your account, so one subscription covers the iPhone app, the Android app and the web app.

Subscriptions are bought in the iPhone or Android app — the web app has no purchase page, so sign in there once and Pro shows up everywhere. Settings shows what your account has and links to the store to manage or cancel it.

Letting Pro lapse never costs you anything you logged. Your events, sessions and lap times stay exactly where they are, you can keep adding more, and resubscribing brings the Pro features back with everything still in place. See the terms for renewal, cancellation and refunds, and note that deleting your account does not cancel the subscription.

Bought the app back when it was a paid download? Pro is yours for life at no further cost — it should appear on its own. If it doesn't, email eric@speedshift.io from the address you sign in with.

Log your first event

An event is a day (or weekend) at a track. Create one, pick the track and layout, and add the sessions you ran with their lap times. Lap times are entered as m:ss.fff (e.g. 2:03.118).

Only kept a best time? That's fine — every event takes an optional manual best time, so spreadsheet-era history can go in as-is and full lap data can be attached later. The track pages chart your best lap per event over time, per track and layout, so improvement trends downward.

Add your cars

Under the account menu, Settings keeps your garage: add each car you track and note its modifications — coilovers, pads, tires, alignment — so setup changes stay part of the story your lap times tell. The event form's Car field suggests cars from your garage, and the one you mark as default is filled in automatically on new events. You can still type any car name directly on an event.

Each car also gets a garage page that tracks its consumables — pads, tires, fluid — with wear projected automatically from the events you log, plus a per-day setup notebook on every event. See Garage & setup notebook.

Import telemetry

On any event page, Import video / telemetry… turns recordings into sessions with laps: Corvette PDR video, GoPro footage, and Racelogic VBO files. Parsing happens on your own device — videos are read in place and never uploaded. See Telemetry import for the full details, including the start/finish line picker.

Import in the browser at trackevolution.app, on a laptop or on your phone — that's where every format is supported. The iPhone and Android apps can also import PDR and GoPro video straight from Files or Photos, which saves copying a clip off the phone first; logger files like VBO are browser-only, so don't go hunting for them in the app.

Telemetry import and the channel graphs it produces are part of Track Evolution Pro.

Get it on your phone

On iPhone and iPad, download Track Evolution from the App Store; on Android, get it from Google Play — it's the same logbook, signed in the same way, plus lap recording from the phone's GPS. On any other device, use the web app.

The web app installs too: open trackevolution.app and use your browser's Add to Home Screen / Install option, and it opens like a native app. Either way your whole logbook keeps working offline — you can browse every event and even log sessions, laps and notes with no signal, and they sync automatically once you're back online (a banner shows what's waiting). On a touch screen, pull down from the top of any page to refresh your data.

Share your progress

You can publish a read-only page of your stats and best times under a slug you choose (trackevolution.app/share/<your-slug>). Notes, your email, and per-lap data are stripped — share the pace, keep the diary private. See share pages for what's included.
