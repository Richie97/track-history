Getting started

Track Evolution lives at trackevolution.app. Sign in with your Google or Apple account and you get your own logbook. This page covers your first event, importing telemetry, and sharing your progress.

For a tour, watch Track Evolution in 84 seconds on YouTube. If you have never driven on track, start with Your first track day.

Sign in

Open trackevolution.app and sign in with Google or Apple. Your first sign-in opens an empty logbook with one button, Add your first event. Everything you record is private to your account. Accounts are matched by email, so Google and Apple sign-ins with the same address reach the same logbook.

Free and Pro

Free, with no limit on how much you log, on all three apps and offline:

  • The logbook: tracks, events, sessions, lap times, best laps, progress charts, consistency and per-session stats.
  • Your cars, prep checklists, share pages and leaderboards.
  • Telemetry import: lap times, the racing line, top speed, max RPM and max lateral G, and the per-lap speed trace, with throttle and brake where the file carries them.

Track Evolution Pro ($1.99/month or $19.99/year) adds the analysis:

  • Recording laps from the phone's GPS.
  • The rest of the channel graphs in imported files (steering, RPM, lateral G and yaw, the gear ribbon, ABS and wheelspin marks, the car-health strip), with sector splits and lap-vs-lap deltas.
  • Consumable wear tracking in the garage.
  • The setup notebook, with its tire-pressure suggestions.
  • What your days cost, rolled up per track and per car, and year in review.
  • Lap video clips.
  • Sharing your logbook with a coach, and connecting an AI assistant.

Pro applies to your account: one subscription covers the iPhone, Android and web apps.

  • On the web, Settings → Subscription offers both plans, with a 14-day free trial the first time. Use Manage billing there to switch plans, update your card, see receipts or cancel.
  • In the iPhone or Android app, a subscription is bought in the app and managed in that store.

Letting Pro lapse never costs you anything you logged. You can keep adding more, and resubscribing brings the Pro features back. See the terms for renewal, cancellation and refunds. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought in a phone app; one bought on the web is cancelled with it.

Bought the app back when it was a paid download? Pro is yours for life at no further cost, and should appear on its own. If it doesn't, email eric@speedshift.io from the address you sign in with.

Log your first event

An event is a day (or weekend) at a track. Create one, pick the track and layout, and add your sessions and their lap times. Lap times are entered as m:ss.fff (e.g. 2:03.118).

The New event form ends with an optional Add laps section: import a video or telemetry file, or type a session's lap times, and they are saved when you tap Create event. To add more later, use the Add a session card on the event page. The phone apps' lap recorder is there too.

If you only kept a best time, enter it as the event's manual best time. Each track page charts your best lap per event, so improvement trends downward.

Pick your units

Under the account menu, Settings → Units switches the logbook between imperial (mph, °F, psi, gallons, miles) and metric (km/h, °C, bar, liters, kilometers). The choice follows your account to every device. It changes how numbers are shown and entered, never what you have logged. New accounts start on imperial.

Add your cars

Open Garage (the link at the top of the page, or the tab in the phone apps) and add each car you track, with its modifications. The event form's Car field suggests your cars and fills in the one you mark as default. You can still type any car name.

Each car has a garage page with its track days and best lap at each track. Pro adds consumable wear (pads, tires, fluid) projected from the events you log, and a per-day setup notebook on every event. See Garage & setup notebook.

Import telemetry

On any event page, Import video / telemetry… turns recordings into sessions with laps: Corvette and Cadillac PDR video, GoPro footage, AiM SmartyCam video, Racelogic VBO files and Porsche Track Precision exports. Files are read on your own device; nothing is uploaded unless you save a lap video with Pro.

The browser, on a laptop or a phone, supports every format. The iPhone and Android apps import PDR, GoPro and SmartyCam video from Files or Photos, and VBO files and Porsche Track Precision exports (VBO or CSV) from Files.

See Telemetry import for the details, including the start/finish line picker, and Free and Pro for which graphs are free.

Get it on your phone

Download Track Evolution from the App Store (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Android). It's the same logbook with the same sign-in, plus lap recording from the phone's GPS. The App Store app also installs on an Apple silicon Mac, without recording. On any other device, use the web app.

The web app installs too, with your browser's Add to Home Screen / Install option at trackevolution.app.

Either way the logbook works offline. Browse every event and log sessions, laps and notes with no signal; they sync once you're back online, and a banner shows what's waiting. On a touch screen, pull down from the top of a page to refresh.

Share your progress

Publish a read-only page of your stats and best times under a slug you choose (trackevolution.app/share/<your-slug>). Notes, your email and per-lap data are left out. On the web, set it up in the dashboard's Share your history panel, which appears once your first event has started. See share pages for what's included.

Share one day or one session

Choose Share on the event page or on a session (on the phones, in the ⋯ menu beside Edit and Delete). You get a link anyone can open without an account, showing that day or session alone: its lap times, racing line, telemetry and best-lap video. Your notes, costs and setup sheets stay private. Stop sharing, in the same place, turns the link off. See what a shared day shows.

To share a single lap or two laps compared:

  • On the phones, open the lap or the lap comparison and choose Share.
  • In the browser, pick a lap, and a second to compare it with, in a session's Share panel. The track's Compare two laps page has its own Share.

See what a shared lap shows.

Share with a coach

With Pro you can give an instructor or coach read-only access to your whole logbook: every event, session and lap, the channel graphs and sector splits, your racing lines, your best-lap videos, and your cars and their modifications.

  1. On trackevolution.app, open Coaching from the account menu (in the iPhone or Android app, Settings → Coaching) and choose Create an invite link.
  2. Send the link to your coach, or in the phone app let them scan its QR code. It works once, for seven days, and is shown only when you create it. Make another if it gets lost.
  3. Your coach opens it, signs in (a free account is enough) and accepts. You'll see them under Coaches who can see your logbook, with when they last looked.

Your coach sees your analysis as far as your own plan includes it, and never your email, notes, prep checklists, costs, setup sheets or garage parts. They can't change anything. They can watch your best-lap videos but not download or delete them. Remove a coach on the Coaching page and their access ends straight away. Coaches has the full list.

Your coach can leave comments on a session, on a lap, or at a marked place on a lap, and you can reply. New ones show on your dashboard. Only you and that coach see them, and you can delete any comment in your logbook. Comments are in the web app and both phone apps.

Fill in your driver profile on the Coaching page (your experience, your safety gear and what you want to work on). Your coach sees it first.

If you coach, your drivers' logbooks are under Your students on your own Coaching page. Each opens read-only, with a banner that says whose it is. See also getting the most from an instructor or coach.

Your season, wrapped

Wrapped tells each season as full-screen cards: your track days, tracks, laps and track miles, the track you kept coming back to, the time you found, your fastest lap, the tracks you drove for the first time, your hours behind the wheel and the hottest day you ran. It is free. Pro adds your favorite tire (the set from your garage with the most track days on it) and your top speed, and where. Swipe, tap the right side or use the arrow keys to move through it.

Track miles are your logged laps times each track's length, from our track list or measured from your own telemetry there. When a length isn't known, the card says how many of your tracks it counted.

  • From year in review: a button opens the year you're looking at ("so far" while the year is still running).
  • From the dashboard: offered from 1 November to the end of January for the season that's ending, in the iPhone and Android apps as well as on the web.
  • Any time, for any year you drove: trackevolution.app/#/wrapped.

The last card is a summary for posting. Share image opens your phone's share sheet with a picture of it, sized for a story. Save image and Save wide download it; the wide one suits a group chat or a forum post. If you have a share page, Copy link copies a public page for that season.

Your data

Download a copy of your logbook at any time from Settings → Your data on the web. There are three files:

  • Laps (CSV): one row per lap, with its event, session, time, whether it was the session's best and whether it was a clean lap.
  • Events (CSV): one row per event, with its best lap, consistency, hours and what it cost.
  • Everything (JSON): your tracks, events, sessions and laps, the comments on them from your coaches and the ones you wrote as a coach, setup sheets, your garage, your profile and preferences, and who you've given access to. Tick Include telemetry to add each session's channel data, which makes the file much larger.

The copy is complete whatever your plan: telemetry, your garage and setup sheets are in it even without Pro. The JSON uses the units the app stores (lap times in milliseconds, speeds in km/h and so on), as the top of the file says.
