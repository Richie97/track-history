How to export Porsche Track Precision laps and data

Porsche's Track Precision app records lap times, GPS and the car's own data on your phone, and can export a session as CSV or VBO. Track Evolution reads both, on the phone or in the browser, so the Porsche's laps and data sit in the same logbook as every other day you've driven.

What you need

  • A session recorded in the Track Precision app, exported from the app as CSV or VBO. Choose CSV when you can: it carries the app's own lap times.
  • A free Track Evolution account, in the iPhone or Android app or on the web at trackevolution.app.

On your phone

  1. Export the session from Track Precision.
  2. Share the file to Track Evolution, or save it to Files, open the event in the Track Evolution app, tap Import video (Import video or VBO… on Android), which takes data files too, and pick it. A VBO export goes by way of Files.
  3. Check the laps in the review, then save.

In the browser

  1. Get the exported file onto your computer.
  2. Open the event at trackevolution.app, or start a New event.
  3. Press Import video / telemetry… and choose the file, or drag it onto the box.
  4. Check the laps in the review, then save.

CSV or VBO: where the lap times come from

ExportLap timesLine to pick?
CSV Track Precision's own lap timer: the times the app showed you No
VBO Timed again from the GPS positions, on the start/finish line the file names. Within a few tenths, marked ~ Only if the file names no line

A CSV lap is kept when the app's timer ran the whole way round, so a lap with a pit stop in it is left out. If the timer never finished a lap, the review shows the track map and you pick the line. See the start/finish line picker.

What you get

  • Lap times for every full lap, and the session's best.
  • Your racing line for the best lap, as a track map.
  • Graphs for every lap, overlaid by distance around the track: speed, throttle, brake, steering, RPM, gear, and cornering and braking force.
  • Tire pressures, lap by lap.

If the car stopped sending data partway through a session, the laps and the racing line still import; the car's graphs stop where the data did.

What is free and what is Pro

  • Free, with no limit on files: importing, the lap times, the racing line, and the speed, throttle and brake graphs for every lap.
  • Track Evolution Pro ($1.99 a month or $19.99 a year): the other graphs, sector splits and a theoretical best lap, lap-against-lap time deltas, the friction circle and the tire pressure readings.

Neither export includes Track Precision's video, so there is no best-lap video to save.

If it doesn't work

  • "Not a Porsche Track Precision CSV". The file is a CSV from something else, or an export without position. Export the session again from Track Precision.
  • "Track Precision CSV contains no usable GPS data". The phone had no GPS position during the session.
  • The review shows a map and no laps. The app's timer finished no lap in this recording. Click the start/finish line on the map.
  • A lap is missing. A lap the timer didn't run through from line to line, such as one with a pit stop, isn't imported.
