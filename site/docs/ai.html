AI assistants

With Track Evolution Pro, you can connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to your logbook and ask it about your driving in plain language: where the time is, how today compared with last year, what to work on next session. It reads your data and can't change anything.

There are two ways in. If you already use Claude or ChatGPT, connect it to your logbook and ask there. Or use the AI Coach inside Track Evolution, on the web, iPhone, iPad and Android, which runs on your own Anthropic or OpenAI account.

What you can ask

  • "Debrief my last session at VIR. Where did I lose time to my best lap?"
  • "Compare my fastest lap this year at Road Atlanta with last year's. Which corners got faster?"
  • "Am I trail braking more than I was in the spring?"
  • "Which setup was I on when I set my best time here?"
  • "When are my front pads due, and what has this car cost me per track day?"
  • "Plan my next day at Barber from my history there."

The assistant works from the same numbers as the app, so its answers can point at a specific lap, corner or figure. Recorded or imported laps give it far more to work with than typed-in times.

Connecting

Every assistant asks for the same address: 

https://trackevolution.app/mcp

Add it as a connector in your assistant. It sends you to Track Evolution to sign in and approve; the approval page says which app is asking and what it can read. Choose Allow.

Connecting Claude

Track Evolution is in Claude's connector directory, with no address to paste:

  1. Open Track Evolution in the Claude directory and choose Connect.
  2. Sign in to Track Evolution and choose Allow.
  3. In a new chat, turn the Track Evolution connector on from the tools menu and ask away.

To add it by hand: in Settings → Connectors, choose Add custom connector, name it Track Evolution and paste the address above.

ChatGPT

In ChatGPT's settings, under Apps & Connectors, create a custom connector with the address above, then sign in and allow it. Custom connectors depend on your ChatGPT plan.

Other assistants

Any assistant that supports remote Model Context Protocol connectors with sign-in can use the same address. In Claude Code, for example: 

claude mcp add --transport http track-evolution https://trackevolution.app/mcp
Menu names in these apps change. If a step doesn't match what you see, look for "connectors" or "MCP servers" in the app's settings.

What it can and can't see

It can read:

  • Your tracks, events, sessions and lap times.
  • Your lap telemetry and the analysis built on it: sector splits and the theoretical best, shift points, ABS and traction-control interventions, grip and balance by corner, and car health.
  • The racing line of each session's fastest lap, measured from the start/finish line, not as map coordinates.
  • Your garage, setup sheets and notes.
  • The leaderboards you can already see in the app: names, best times and dates.

It can't:

  • Add, change or delete anything.
  • Read the comments between you and a coach.
  • See other drivers' logbooks.

Your data and the assistant

Nothing is sent to an assistant until you connect it and ask it something. Its app then receives the parts of your logbook it asks for, and handles them under that provider's terms and your account with them. See the privacy policy.

Disconnecting

In the Track Evolution web app, open the account menu → Settings. AI assistants lists each connected assistant, when you connected it and when it was last used. Disconnect cuts it off immediately. Remove the connector in the assistant too.

Pro

Connecting an assistant and the AI Coach are both part of Track Evolution Pro. If your subscription lapses, neither can read your logbook until you resubscribe. Nothing in your logbook changes, and replies you saved to your notes stay there.

The AI Coach

The AI Coach is the same kind of debrief without leaving Track Evolution. It is part of Pro, in the web app and the iPhone, iPad and Android apps. On the web and on a tablet it opens beside the page you are on, so the charts it talks about stay in view; on a phone it opens as a sheet over the page. It is offered in three places:

  • Debrief this session, on each session of an event: what went well, where the time is, and one or two things to try next.
  • Explain the difference, on the two-lap comparison: where the time went between the two laps you picked, corner by corner.
  • What should I work on next time?, on an event: a plan for your next visit, from the day and your history at the track.

You can ask follow-up questions, and keep a reply with Save to notes: a session debrief is added to that session's notes, the others to the event's. A saved reply is marked AI Coach with the date, so you (and a coach you share your logbook with) can tell it from your own notes.

Setting it up

The AI Coach runs on your own account with Anthropic (the maker of Claude) or OpenAI (the maker of ChatGPT), through an API key. That is a separate account from a Claude or ChatGPT subscription; if a subscription is what you have, connecting your assistant is the easier way.

  1. Create an API key with Anthropic or OpenAI. Both ask you to add credit to the account first.
  2. In the Track Evolution web app, open the account menu → Settings → AI assistants → AI Coach. In the iPhone or Android app, open Settings → AI assistants.
  3. Paste the key beside its provider and choose Verify. If you add a key for each, pick which one the AI Coach uses.
  4. Optionally choose a Model: start typing and pick from the models your account with that provider offers. Leave it empty to use the default.

On the web, a key is kept for the browser tab you typed it into. Tick Remember on this device to store it in that browser instead, until you choose Forget key or sign out. In the iPhone and Android apps, the key is stored in that phone's secure storage until you forget it or sign out, and it isn't included in backups or carried to a new phone. Either way it is never stored on our servers, and a key added on one device isn't on your others.

What it costs

Track Evolution doesn't charge for the AI Coach beyond Pro. You pay Anthropic or OpenAI directly for what each question uses, at their prices, which depend on the model you pick. You can set a spending limit in your account with them.

What is sent

When you ask the AI Coach something, the parts of your logbook it needs to answer are sent to the provider you chose, under your own account with them: the same things an assistant can read, as summaries and figures, not your video. Your key travels with the request and is not kept. The AI Coach only reads; it can't add, change or delete anything, and it only ever works on your own logbook. See the privacy policy.

A word on coaching

An assistant, or the AI Coach, is a second pair of eyes on your data, not an instructor. Its corner numbers (T1, T2…) are the app's own, counted from the start/finish line, not the circuit's official turn numbers. Treat its suggestions as things to try next session, talk them through with your coach, and never use it while you're driving.

The AI Coach is not the same thing as sharing your logbook with a coach. That is a person you invite, who reads your sessions and leaves comments on them. The AI Coach never sees those comments, and a coach reading your logbook can't run the AI Coach on it.
