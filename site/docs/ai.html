AI assistants

With Track Evolution Pro, you can connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to your logbook and ask it about your driving in plain language: where the time is, how today compared with last year, what to work on next session. It reads your data and can't change anything.

What you can ask

The assistant sees what the app sees, so it can answer questions like:

  • "Debrief my last session at VIR. Where did I lose time to my best lap?"
  • "Compare my fastest lap this year at Road Atlanta with last year's. Which corners got faster?"
  • "Am I trail braking more than I was in the spring?"
  • "Which setup was I on when I set my best time here?"
  • "When are my front pads due, and what has this car cost me per track day?"
  • "Plan my next day at Barber from my history there."

It works from the same numbers as the app — lap times, sector splits and the theoretical best, shift points, ABS and traction-control interventions, grip and balance by corner, car health, your garage and your setup sheets — so its answers can point at a specific lap, corner or figure. Laps recorded with the app or imported from telemetry give it far more to work with than typed-in times.

Connecting

Every assistant asks for the same address: 

https://trackevolution.app/mcp

Add it as a connector in your assistant, and it will send you to Track Evolution to sign in and approve the connection. The approval page says which app is asking and what it will be able to read; choose Allow and you're sent back.

Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Track Evolution and paste the address above.
  3. Choose Connect, sign in to Track Evolution, and choose Allow.
  4. In a new chat, turn the Track Evolution connector on from the tools menu and ask away.

ChatGPT

In ChatGPT's settings, under Apps & Connectors, create a custom connector with the address above, then sign in and allow it the same way. Custom connectors depend on your ChatGPT plan.

Other assistants

Any assistant that supports remote connectors — the kind that use the Model Context Protocol and sign you in — can use the same address. In Claude Code, for example: 

claude mcp add --transport http track-evolution https://trackevolution.app/mcp
Menu names in these apps change as they update; if a step above doesn't match what you see, look for "connectors" or "MCP servers" in the app's settings.

What it can and can't see

  • It can read your tracks, events, sessions and lap times, your lap telemetry and the analysis built on it, the racing line of each session's fastest lap (the same one the track map draws, measured from the start/finish line rather than as map coordinates), your garage, setup sheets and notes, and the leaderboards you can already see in the app.
  • It can't add, change or delete anything.
  • It can't see other drivers' logbooks. On a leaderboard it sees exactly what you see: names, best times and dates.

Your data and the assistant

Nothing is sent to an assistant until you connect it and ask it something. Then the assistant's app receives the parts of your logbook it asks for to answer you, and handles them under that provider's terms and your account with them — the same as anything else you paste into a chat. See the privacy policy.

Disconnecting

Open Settings in the Track Evolution web app (account menu → Settings). AI assistants lists every assistant you've connected, when you connected it and when it was last used; Disconnect cuts it off immediately. Remove the connector in the assistant too, so it stops offering it.

Pro

Connecting an assistant is part of Track Evolution Pro. If your subscription lapses, the assistant is told Pro is needed and can't read your logbook until you resubscribe; nothing in your logbook changes either way.

A word on coaching

An assistant is a sharp second pair of eyes on your data, not an instructor. Its corner numbers (T1, T2…) are the app's own, counted from the start/finish line, not the circuit's official turn numbers. Treat its suggestions as things to try in the next session, talk them through with your coach, and never use an assistant while you're driving.
