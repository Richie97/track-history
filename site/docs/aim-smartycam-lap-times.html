How to get lap times from AiM SmartyCam video

An AiM SmartyCam wired to an AiM dash or logger stores the logger's data inside the video, alongside the picture it draws it over. Track Evolution reads that data from the camera's .MOV file and imports the logger's own lap times, the ones on your dash, with no start/finish line to set. The video stays on your computer or phone.

What you need

  • The camera's video files (.MOV) from its memory card. One recording is one session.
  • Lap timing working on the AiM dash or logger during the session. The laps come from it, so a recording made while it timed no laps has none to import.
  • A free Track Evolution account, on the web at trackevolution.app or in the iPhone or Android app.

It is the camera's video that is read. AiM's logger files themselves (from Race Studio) aren't.

In the browser

  1. Put the camera's memory card in your computer, or copy the videos off it.
  2. Open the event at trackevolution.app, or start a New event.
  3. Press Import video / telemetry… and choose the videos, or drag them onto the box. Each file becomes its own session.
  4. Check the laps in the review, then save.

On your phone

  1. Get the videos where the phone can see them, for example on a card reader that shows up in Files.
  2. Open the event in the Track Evolution app and tap Import video, then pick the clips. Or share a clip to Track Evolution from Files (Open with on iPhone, Share on Android).
  3. Check the laps in the review, then save.

Where the lap times come from

From the AiM logger. Each lap arrives with the time the logger gave it, so your logbook matches your dash and nothing is estimated.

A lap is imported when both of its line crossings are in the video. That leaves out the out-lap, and a lap cut off by the end of the recording.

What you get

  • Lap times for every full lap in the video, and the session's best.
  • Your racing line for the best lap, drawn as a track map.
  • Graphs for every lap, laid over each other by distance around the track: speed, and the RPM, throttle and gear the camera stored.

Which car readings a file carries depends on how the camera and logger are set up. A reading the file doesn't have, or one that doesn't look right, is left out and the rest still imports.

What is free and what is Pro

Importing is free, with no limit on files: the lap times, the racing line, and the speed and throttle graphs for every lap. Track Evolution Pro ($1.99 a month or $19.99 a year, one subscription across the web, iPhone and Android) adds the RPM and gear graphs with your shift points, sector splits and a theoretical best lap, and lap-against-lap time deltas.

Best-lap video isn't saved from SmartyCam files yet.

If it doesn't work

  • "This recording wasn't finished by the camera and can't be read." The camera lost power before it closed the file. That recording can't be imported; the others on the card can.
  • The session has no laps, or fewer than you drove. Only laps the logger timed, with both crossings in the video, are imported. A recording that starts after you crossed the line loses its first lap, and one made while the logger timed no laps has none.
  • "No GPS data in this SmartyCam video". The logger had no GPS position during the recording.
  • "No PDR, SmartyCam or GoPro telemetry in this video". The file isn't the camera's original. A video that was edited or re-encoded has lost its data; use the one from the card.
