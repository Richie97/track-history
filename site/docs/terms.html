Terms of use

Track Evolution (trackevolution.app) is a personal track-day logbook operated by Speedshift LLC. The logbook is free; some features need a Track Evolution Pro subscription. By using it you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy. Effective September 2, 2026.

The service

Track Evolution lets you record track-day events, sessions, lap times and notes, chart your progress, import telemetry, and optionally publish a read-only share page. It requires signing in with a Google or Apple account. The logbook is free; the features listed under subscriptions need Track Evolution Pro.

Subscriptions and billing

Recording laps, importing telemetry, channel graphs, consumable tracking in the garage, the setup notebook and year in review are part of Track Evolution Pro. Everything else — tracks, events, sessions, lap times, best laps, progress charts, consistency, share pages and leaderboards — is free, with no limit on how much you log.

  • Where you buy it. Pro is sold only through the App Store and Google Play, in the iPhone and Android apps. It applies to your account, so it covers all three apps and this site.
  • Auto-renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each term at the price shown at purchase, until cancelled. The store charges you and handles renewals; we never see your payment details.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time in your Apple ID subscriptions or in Google Play. Cancelling stops the next renewal — Pro runs to the end of the term you've already paid for. Deleting your Track Evolution account does not cancel a store subscription; see deleting your account.
  • Refunds are handled by the store that sold the subscription, under its own policy — Apple for the App Store, Google for Google Play. We can't issue them ourselves.
  • Free trials and introductory offers, where offered, convert to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel first.
  • Price changes apply from a future renewal, never retroactively, and the store notifies you first as its rules require.
  • If Pro lapses — cancelled, expired, or a payment that didn't go through — you keep everything you've logged. Nothing is deleted, nothing is locked away, and you can still add events, sessions and lap times, and export or share as before. The Pro features simply stop until you resubscribe, at which point everything reappears exactly as it was.
  • If you bought the app when it was a paid download, Pro is yours permanently at no further cost.

Your content

Everything you log stays yours. By using the service you give us permission to store and process that data for one purpose: operating the service and showing your logbook back to you. Nothing you record is made public unless you enable a share page — and then only the stats a share page includes, under the slug you chose.

Acceptable use

You agree to use Track Evolution lawfully and reasonably. In particular, don't:

  • attempt to access another user's logbook or account;
  • disrupt, overload, probe or interfere with the service;
  • choose a share slug that impersonates someone else or misleads;
  • enter content you don't have the right to store or publish.

If the service were ever to shut down, we'd make reasonable efforts to give notice so you can copy your data out. If a feature covered by an active Pro subscription is withdrawn, contact us and we'll sort it out.
