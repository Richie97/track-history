How to get lap times from Cadillac Blackwing PDR video

The Performance Data Recorder (PDR) in Cadillac's V-Series Blackwing cars times laps itself and saves them, with the car's data, inside the video file. Track Evolution reads that file on your own computer or phone and brings the recorder's lap times in exactly as the car timed them, with no start/finish line to set.

What you need

  • The PDR's video files (.mp4) from the car's SD card. One recording is one session.
  • A free Track Evolution account, on the web at trackevolution.app or in the iPhone or Android app.
  • An event in your logbook for the day, or make one as you import.

In the browser

  1. Put the PDR's SD card in your computer, or copy the videos off it.
  2. Open the event at trackevolution.app, or start a New event.
  3. Press Import video / telemetry… and choose the videos, or drag them onto the box. Each file becomes its own session.
  4. Check the laps in the review, then save.

On your phone

  1. Get the videos where the phone can see them, for example on a USB stick or card reader that shows up in Files.
  2. Open the event in the Track Evolution app and tap Import video, then pick the clips. Or share a clip to Track Evolution from Files (Open with on iPhone, Share on Android).
  3. Check the laps in the review, then save.

The video is read where it sits, and it is never uploaded.

Where the lap times come from

From the recorder's own lap timer. Each lap the car timed arrives with the time the car gave it, to the millisecond, so the numbers in your logbook match the ones the car showed you.

If the car's timer wasn't timing laps during a recording, the file has none to import. The review then shows the track map you drove: click where the start/finish line is and laps are timed on each pass across it, to within a few tenths of a second and marked with a ~. See the start/finish line picker.

What you get

  • Lap times for every timed lap, and the session's best.
  • Your racing line for the best lap, drawn as a track map.
  • Top speed, max RPM and max lateral G for the session.
  • Graphs for every lap, laid over each other by distance around the track: speed, throttle, brake, steering, RPM and gear, cornering and braking force, and where ABS or traction control cut in.
  • The car's health, lap by lap: oil, coolant and transmission temperatures, oil pressure, tire pressures and temperatures, and fuel. This recorder doesn't log battery voltage.

What is free and what is Pro

Importing is free, with no limit on files: the lap times, the racing line, the session's top figures, and the speed, throttle and brake graphs for every lap. Track Evolution Pro ($1.99 a month or $19.99 a year, one subscription across the web, iPhone and Android) adds the other graphs, sector splits and a theoretical best lap, lap-against-lap time deltas, the friction circle, the car-health readings and best-lap video: a clip of your fastest lap, or of every lap, kept with the session.

If it doesn't work

  • The review shows a map and no laps. The car's lap timer timed none in this recording. Click the start/finish line on the map.
  • "No laps cross the picked line". Click a different spot, on a part of the track the car drove on every lap.
  • "No PDR, SmartyCam or GoPro telemetry in this video". The file has no data track. A video that was re-encoded, trimmed in an editor or downloaded from a sharing site has lost it; use the original from the SD card.
